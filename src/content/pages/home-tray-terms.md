We accept returns only in the case of a defective or damaged Home Tray. If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us within 15 days of receiving your order to arrange for a replacement.

Please note that we do not accept returns for reasons other than product defects. Additionally, items must be returned in their original packaging and in unused condition to qualify for a replacement.

**No refunds. Replacements only.**

If you have any questions, please email [support@overyondr.com](mailto:support@overyondr.com).
