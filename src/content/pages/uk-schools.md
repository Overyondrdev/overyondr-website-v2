Since 2014, we have led the charge to create phone-free schools, supporting thousands of pupils across the United Kingdom every day. With UK-based partner support and implementation, Yondr is proud to work with schools to help pupils experience the focus, creativity, and relief that comes with a phone-free education.

## Our leading approach

At Yondr, we believe the pouch is just one piece of the puzzle. The Yondr Phone-Free Schools Programme includes a suite of resources designed to ensure a seamless transition, based on 10+ years experience working with thousands of educators around the world.

Our leading approach empowers schools:

- Policy Consultation and Development
- Operational Planning and Equipment
- Training and Ongoing Support

Our unique, lockable pouch means students keep their phones, but cannot access them during the school day. Teachers no longer have to be the phone police, and pupils get a much needed 6-8 hour break from digital distractions.

## Phone-free focus. Proven results.

Yondr schools report dramatic improvements in academic performance, classroom engagement, and social connection, alongside a significant decrease in cyberbullying and behavioral incidents.

Classrooms are focused. Pupils are less anxious. The playground is loud and lively. Without the constant inundation of technology, kids can be kids again.
