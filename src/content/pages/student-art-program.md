Selected work will be printed on Yondr gear such as posters, bandanas, playing cards, bookmarks, stickers and more! If your art is selected, you and your school will receive your newly printed art to share with friends and family.

## Creative direction

- Submit original artwork of any kind that you have created – in any form
- We want to see what inspires you – no particular subject matter
- If you need support with art materials, email us at [art@overyondr.com](mailto:art@overyondr.com)
- Submissions will be accepted and evaluated on a rolling basis
