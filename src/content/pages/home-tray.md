## FAQ

### How does this product work?

The Yondr Home Tray is a signal-blocking, lockable box to store phones and other smart devices. Place it in a convenient location of your choosing.

### Where can the Yondr Home Tray be used?

- **Workplaces** – Increase office productivity and privacy.
- **Hotels / Spas** – Make each guest room a phone-free oasis.
- **Other** – Transform any space into a phone-free zone. The Yondr Home Tray is portable and requires no batteries or power sources for operation.

### How does the Home Tray block signal?

The Home Tray uses faraday fabric lining on the interior of the box that blocks all incoming and outgoing signal. Faraday fabric is made with copper and nickel, allowing it to block 5G, Wi-Fi, cell phone signal, Bluetooth, GPS and RFID. Please note that the Home Tray will only block signal if the back hatch is fully closed. If you choose to keep it open in order to thread power cables through to charge your devices, the Home Tray will not block signal. When not blocking signal, the Home Tray is still a convenient place to store your technology and keep phones out of sight and out of mind.

### How many devices / what type of devices can the Home Tray hold?

The Home Tray is designed to hold smartphones and smartwatches. Approximately six smartphones can fit very comfortably inside.

### How does the Home Tray lock?

You do not need to lock the Home Tray in order to enjoy its signal-blocking benefits. However, many parents find that locking the Home Tray helps to better control children’s screen time. If you do choose to lock it, simply use the small key provided to manually turn the lock located under the leather tab on the front.
