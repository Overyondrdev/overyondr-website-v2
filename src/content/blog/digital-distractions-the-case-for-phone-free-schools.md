Yondr CEO, Graham Dugoni joined Vertex Partnership Academies in a discussion “Digital Distractions: The Case for Phone-Free Schools.” The speakers also included Jonathan Haidt, renowned social psychologist and author; Ian Rowe, Senior fellow at AEI and CEO of Vertex Partnership Academies; Doug Lemov, Author and Educator; and Lyne James Turnbull, Founding Principal, New Pathways Academy.

Watch Yondr CEO Graham Dugoni speak on the positive effects of phone-free schools. The full discussion is available [here](https://www.youtube.com/live/5BTg5sl0iO0?si=ahfRO-92LT-_isH7).
