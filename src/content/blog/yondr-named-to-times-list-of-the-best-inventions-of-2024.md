We are incredibly proud to announce that [Yondr](/) has been named to [TIME’s Best Inventions of 2024 list](https://time.com/7094783/yondr-pouch/)! The list includes 200 groundbreaking inventions that are changing the ways we live, work, play, and think about what’s possible.

Yondr is the industry leader and inventor of phone-free spaces, with 10 years of experience developing rigorously tested products and offering unparalleled support, expertise, and guidance to all of our partners.

"We are thrilled to be recognized by Time as the inventor of phone-free spaces," said Graham Dugoni, Yondr founder and CEO. "When I founded Yondr 10 years ago, the world was convinced that we needed more, not less, technology in our lives. A decade on, it has become clear that people desperately need protected places where conversation can thrive and creativity can flourish without the distraction of phones and social media. At Yondr, our mission is to continue creating spaces where people can disconnect from their phones and truly connect with each other."

A mission-driven organization for over a decade, we work closely with schools, artists, venues, courtrooms, and more. Yondr patented pouches allow users to be phone-free while still maintaining possession of phones and wearable tech at all times. We pair our products with training, tools, and support programs tailored to the needs of different settings.

From [school districts](/spaces/education) to weddings to [sold-out arena tours](/spaces/live-events) around the world, Yondr provides customized solutions to create phone-free experiences that are truly transformative.
