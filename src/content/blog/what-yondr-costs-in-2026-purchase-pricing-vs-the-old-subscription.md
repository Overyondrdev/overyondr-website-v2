Districts researching Yondr keep running into two outdated ideas: that Yondr is a subscription based model (based on a per-student subscription), and that it's priced the way it was priced years ago. Neither reflects the reality of how Yondr sells today.

The Yondr Program is sold on a purchase model that includes everything a client needs to implement their phone free program including the pouches, locking equipment, and the onboarding and support to get started and keep the program working. From that point on, the school owns it all outright. While the average cost of these program implementations are often $22-$25 per student, Yondr’s US contracts and UK frameworks allow for negotiable, volume-based pricing as well.

## What "Purchase Model" Actually Means

The price a school pays for the comprehensive implementation program is inclusive of so much more than the pouch itself:

- Policy consultation and development
- Pre-implementation checklists and communication exhibits for key stakeholders
- Implementation and system roll-out
- Staff onboarding training on the system and the policy behind it
- Ongoing partner success support for the school
- Pouches and unlocking accessories

That's the practical difference between buying a pouch and buying a program. A vendor that sells only the hardware leaves a school to build the policy, train staff, and manage parent communication on its own. Yondr's complete program pricing model bundles all that work in from the start.

## Why Comparing Pouches To Programs Can Be A Mistake

Because the purchase is one-time, the number a school sees when it first buys in isn't a number that recurs every year. In fact Yondr pouches are built to last and be reused by a student for years. Therefore, a school only reorders for new students entering the program or for lost and damaged units.

That matters most when a school or district is comparing a Yondr full program launch pricing against a competitor's advertised per-student pouch price. They are comparing apples and oranges. A one-time product only purchase versus full program implementation purchases that include the product can look like a deal because a product only purchase does not account for all the many costs of implementing a successful program. A successful rollout involves administration labor, design policy, training of staff, logistics setup, communicating with stakeholders, preparing for handling exceptions, initial onboarding, and so much more. These costs are both hidden and opportunity costs that a product only purchase does not account for.

## Where the Confusion Comes From

Two claims circulate in the market that don't reflect Yondr's current model:

- Outdated claim number one: Yondr is a recurring, per-student subscription. It isn't. The subscription and rental models Yondr used previously have been retired.
- Outdated claim number two: Yondr's pricing is expensive relative to what a district gets. The Yondr program provides everything a district or school needs to implement a successful phone free program. The program’s pricing is negotiable and volume-based, and it's inclusive of training, implementation, and ongoing support rather than hardware alone.

Competitors citing the retired subscription model, or comparing only a price per pouch without accounting for what's included, are describing a version of Yondr that no longer exists.

## What Drives the Number for Your School

Because pricing is negotiable and volume-based, the final number depends on factors specific to a school or district rather than a fixed per-unit rate:

- Number of students the program will cover
- Whether the deployment is a single school or a full district rollout
- Whether the signal-blocking configuration is included alongside the standard pouch
- The scope of training and ongoing support a district wants built in

The most reliable way to get an accurate number is a direct conversation with Yondr's team, since published per-unit figures elsewhere in the market don't reflect how volume pricing actually works.

## FAQs

### Is Yondr still a subscription?

No. Yondr is sold on a purchase model. Schools buy the pouches and program once and own the pouches outright.

### Does the price include anything besides the pouches?

Yes. Yondr's pricing model is inclusive of pouches, locking equipment, staff training, ongoing support for teachers, students, and parents, implementation, system roll-out, and policy consultation.

### Do we have to buy new pouches every year?

No. Pouches are built to be reused across the years a student is enrolled. Reorders are typically limited to new students entering the program and to lost or damaged units, not a full annual repurchase.

### How is the price determined?

Pricing is negotiable and volume-based, driven by factors like student count, whether the rollout is district-wide, and whether the signal-blocking configuration is included. A direct conversation with Yondr is the most accurate way to get a number for your school.
