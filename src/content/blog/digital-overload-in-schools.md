What do academic performance, mental health, and social skills have in common? They're all under siege, casualties of our very real over-reliance on smartphones.

The profound impact of smartphones cannot be understated. But what once was a revolutionary tool for communication and connection has proven to have equally profound consequences.

### Here’s a brief recap of the latest findings:

> 41% of teenagers feel overwhelmed by the number of text alerts they receive on a daily basis.
>
> —SlickText

> The risk level of smartphone addiction in adolescents is 25.5%, significantly higher than the 8.9% risk level for adults.
>
> —Elsevier Science Direct

### What’s Working to Reduce Digital Overload

Citing benefits like improved focus, reduced cyberbullying, and enhanced face-to-face social interaction, the phone-free schools movement is gaining momentum.

To date, [31 states](/school-phone-bans) have now enacted bans or limits on phone usage for the 2025–26 school year, many with the help of Yondr’s [Phone-Free Schools Program](/spaces/education).
