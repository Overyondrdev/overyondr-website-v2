Cell phones and social media are increasingly disrupting student focus, performance, and mental health. In response, the [U.S. Department of Education](https://www.ed.gov/about/news/press-release/us-department-of-education-calls-states-districts-and-schools-adopt-policies-student-use-of-cell-phones-and-personal-devices) is urging the adoption of clear, research-based policies for cell phone use in schools, emphasizing the importance of creating policies that meet the unique needs of each school community. A growing number of [states](https://www.edweek.org/technology/which-states-ban-or-restrict-cellphones-in-schools/2024/06) have implemented legislation or unlocked funding to restrict the use of cell phones and wearable tech in schools. In addition, districts and schools have implemented their own phone-use restrictions.

**However, not all phone policies are effective; some are too vague or difficult to enforce, making a clear, consistent policy—and program—essential.**

> **_“Cellphone bans are in place in 77% of schools. But this high number seems to refer to... any school that tells students they should not use their phone while in class. That's not really a ban; it's more of an unenforceable wish.”_**
>
> - Jonathan Haidt, Author and Social Psychologist,\
>   [_The Atlantic: Get Phones Out of Schools Now_](https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2023/06/ban-smartphones-phone-free-schools-social-media/674304/)

**Yondr’s All-Day Solution**

More than just a pouch, The [Yondr Phone-Free Schools Program](/spaces/education) is a complete bell-to-bell solution combining patented technology, tailored training, and ongoing support.

- **Phone-Free Equipment**: Essential tools like pouches, unlocking bases, and lockboxes make the transition to a phone-free environment seamless.
- **Training & Support:** Year-round program support, including pre-launch planning, mid-year check-ins, and end-of-year reviews, ensures success every step of the way.
- **Curriculum Resources:** Customizable presentations, lesson plans, and activity guides help educators and parents build healthy digital habits beyond the classroom.

Yondr schools report dramatic improvements in academic performance, classroom engagement, and social connection, alongside a significant decrease in cyberbullying and behavioral incidents. Many teachers at Yondr schools have reported gaining as much as [10 minutes of additional teaching and learning time each lesson](https://communityimpact.com/dallas-fort-worth/richardson/education/2022/11/10/richardson-isd-cellphone-policy-seeing-teacher-support-concern-from-parents-according-to-survey/?utm_source=chatgpt.com). In addition to reported benefits in learning and behavioral outcomes for students, Yondr schools have reported [improved work environments](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/brockton-high-school-locking-up-student-phones/) for staff.

Yondr’s patented lockable pouch allows students to maintain ownership of their devices while reinforcing boundaries for a focused and supportive learning environment. While phone bans and cell phone collection methods are often equated with punishment or being in trouble, Yondr’s all-day solution makes a positive difference for students in a much less confrontational way. With Yondr, students are not tempted to break policy, lose focus, and potentially suffer school consequences. They don’t worry about missing out on the digital world because their classmates’ phones are out of sight and out of mind, too. Instead, they get a much needed 6-8 hour break from digital distractions.
