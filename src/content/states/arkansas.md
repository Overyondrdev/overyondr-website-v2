Educators around the world are seeing the damaging effects of phones on student engagement, social development, and mental health. Yondr is proud to work with the state of Arkansas to help students experience the focus, creativity, and relief that comes with a phone-free education.

## A Groundbreaking Initiative

Yondr is proud to participate in the statewide phone-free schools initiative announced by Governor Sanders of Arkansas. This initiative will provide Yondr’s proven products and program to any secondary school in the state of Arkansas who wishes to institute a full-day phone-free learning environment. This proactive measure aims to combat student screen addiction and social media distractions, in response to concerning trends in youth mental health and academic performance nationwide.

[**Read the full press release**](https://governor.arkansas.gov/news_post/sanders-invites-arkansas-school-districts-to-join-phone-free-school-mental-health-pilot-program/)

> “This isn't about taking anything away. This is about giving students the freedom to enjoy a phone-free education.”
>
> — Governor Sanders
